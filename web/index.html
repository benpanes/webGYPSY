About this app

webGYPSY runs the GYPSY growth and yield model (Alberta, May 2009 functions) in your browser. Your data stay on your computer: nothing is uploaded. The model is the C++ core of the rGYPSY R package, which reproduces the GYPSY SAS program and was validated against it on 7,333 test stands.
